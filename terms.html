1. Who we are

Hololand is a clothing brand from Chittagong, Bangladesh, selling panjabis, knitwear and more at hololandbd.com and from our store. “We”, “us” and “Hololand” mean the Hololand business; “you” means the person visiting the site or placing an order. You can reach us on WhatsApp at our number.

2. Placing an order

  • When you place an order on the website you get an order number straight away. We then call or message you to confirm it.
  • An order is accepted once we confirm it. We may decline or cancel an order if an item is out of stock, if a price was shown wrongly, if we can’t reach you or verify the delivery details, or if an order looks fake or abusive. If you have already paid, we refund you in full (see Returns & Refunds).
  • For a phone number that has refused or returned parcels before, we may ask for payment in advance before sending a new order.
  • You can follow any order at Track your order with your order number and phone number.

3. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Bangladeshi Taka (৳). The delivery charge is shown separately at checkout before you order, and the total you see at checkout is what you pay.
  • Sale prices apply until the end date shown, and only to the items marked as on sale.
  • You can pay cash on delivery, or online where offered at checkout. Online payments (bKash, Nagad, Rocket and cards) are processed by SSLCommerz, a licensed payment gateway in Bangladesh. We never see or store your card number, PIN or wallet password.
  • Promo codes: one code per order, subject to the conditions shown with the code (minimum order, last day, number of uses). Codes have no cash value. A code’s discount applies to the items, not to delivery.

4. Delivery

  • We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh through a courier partner.
  • Delivery charge: ৳70 inside Chittagong city and ৳130 outside Chittagong, free on orders over ৳5,000.
  • Usual delivery time: 1–2 working days inside Chittagong city and 2–4 working days outside. These are estimates, not guarantees; weather, holidays and courier delays can make an order later.
  • Please give a complete address and a phone number that will be answered. If a parcel can’t be delivered because the address is wrong or nobody answers, we may charge the delivery cost before sending it again.
  • Please check the parcel when it arrives. With cash on delivery you pay the courier the amount due.

5. Exchanges, returns and refunds

You can exchange an item for another size or colour within 7 days of delivery if it is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Damaged, faulty or wrong items are replaced or refunded. The full rules are on our Returns & Refunds page.

6. Products

  • We do our best to show colours, fabrics and details accurately, but colours can look slightly different on different screens. Embroidery and handwork can vary a little from piece to piece.
  • Size guides and “Find my size” suggestions are a guide. If you’re unsure, message us before ordering.

7. Our online stylist

The stylist chat, size suggestions and gift ideas on the site are answered automatically using AI, based on our products and our FAQ. They are there to help you choose, but they can make mistakes. Prices, stock and anything about your order are always confirmed by our team, and the details on the product page and at checkout are what count.
