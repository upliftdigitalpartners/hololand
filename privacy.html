Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and who sees it. We keep it to what we need to get your order to you.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyReturns & Refunds

1. When you order

To take and deliver your order we ask for your name, mobile number, delivery area and address, and anything you choose to add: a note, a gift message, a promo code, and, if you pay online, an email address for the payment receipt. We also keep what you ordered, the amounts and the order’s status.

We use these details to confirm your order by phone or message, deliver it, handle exchanges and refunds, and answer your questions. We also look at the order history of a phone number (for example, parcels refused before) to protect the shop from fake orders.

2. Payments

Online payments are made on the secure page of SSLCommerz, our payment gateway. Your card number, PIN and wallet details go to SSLCommerz and your bank or wallet, never to us. We only receive confirmation of the payment: the amount, the method (for example bKash or Visa), and a transaction reference.

3. Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We share only what each service needs:

  • Our courier partner (currently Steadfast Courier): your name, phone number, address and the amount to collect, so they can deliver your parcel.
  • SSLCommerz, if you pay online: your name, phone number, address, email (if given) and the order amount, as required to process the payment.
  • Our team’s phones: new orders are sent to our staff through Telegram so we can confirm them quickly.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), for our ads: see “Advertising” below.
  • Our service providers: the website and order records are hosted with Cloudflare and GitHub.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

4. Our online stylist

When you use the stylist chat, size helper, gift finder or photo match, the messages, voice notes and photos you send are passed to our AI provider (Groq) to produce an answer. We don’t keep chat messages, voice notes or photos on our servers after answering. Please don’t share personal details in the chat that you don’t want processed this way.

5. Visit statistics

We count visits to understand what customers look at: pages and products viewed, items added to the bag, device type (phone or computer), approximate city and country, and the site you came from. Our own statistics are anonymous: they use no cookies, and no names, phone numbers or IP addresses are stored. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, you are not counted.

6. Advertising (Meta Pixel)

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. To show our ads to people likely to be interested and to see which ads lead to orders, the site uses the Meta Pixel. It sets a Meta cookie in your browser and tells Meta which products you view, what you add to your bag, when you start checkout and when you order (the items and the total).

When you order, we also send Meta your phone number and name in hashed (scrambled) form, the city if you’re in Chittagong, and your browser type and IP address, so Meta can match the order to an ad you saw. For orders paid online, these details are kept with the order only until the payment comes through, then deleted.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track”, the Pixel is not loaded. You can also manage how Meta uses this in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

7. Stored on your own device

Apart from Meta’s cookie (above), your browser keeps your bag, your light/dark theme, the order numbers (with the phone number) of orders placed on that device, so the Track page can list them (tap “Forget these” to remove them) and, only if you tick “Remember my details” at checkout, your name, phone and address, so you don’t have to type them again. These stay on your device and you can clear them at any time (untick the box, or clear your browser’s site data).

The site also asks a free weather service (Open-Meteo) for the weather in Chittagong to suggest what to wear. That request doesn’t include any of your personal details.

8. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for deliveries, exchanges, refunds, accounts and our legal obligations. Payment and courier companies keep their own records under their own policies.

9. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it when we no longer need it for an order or the law. Message us on WhatsApp from the number you ordered with.

10. Security

The site uses HTTPS, order records are kept on secure servers, and only our team can open them, with a password.

11. Changes

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
