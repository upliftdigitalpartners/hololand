Returns & Refunds

Wrong size? Something not right? Here’s how exchanges, cancellations and refunds work.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyReturns & Refunds

1. Exchanges (size or colour)

  • You can exchange an item for another size or colour within 7 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.
  • Exchanges depend on the size or colour you want being in stock. If it isn’t, you can choose another item.
  • For a size or colour change, the delivery charge for sending the new item is paid by you.

2. Damaged, faulty or wrong items

  • If your item arrives damaged or faulty, or isn’t what you ordered, message us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo.
  • We’ll replace it, or refund it in full if a replacement isn’t possible, and we cover the delivery both ways.

3. How to ask

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number (it starts with HL-) and what you’d like to do.
  2. We’ll tell you how to send the item back, or arrange a pickup.
  3. Once we receive and check the item, we send the exchange or start the refund.

4. Cancelling an order

  • Before it’s shipped: message us and we’ll cancel it free of charge. If you paid online, you get a full refund.
  • After it’s shipped: it can’t be cancelled, but you can use an exchange or return as above once it arrives.
  • An online order that isn’t paid within about an hour is cancelled automatically. Nothing is charged.
  • Refusing a cash-on-delivery parcel without a reason costs us the courier charge. For numbers that do this, we may ask for advance payment on future orders.

5. Refunds

You get a refund when:

  • an item is damaged, faulty or wrong and can’t be replaced;
  • you paid but we can’t send your order (for example, it’s out of stock), or we cancel it;
  • you cancel an order you paid for before it’s shipped.

How refunds are paid: payments made online through SSLCommerz (bKash, Nagad, Rocket or card) are refunded to the same account or card. Other payments are refunded by bKash or Nagad to your number.

How long it takes: we start the refund within 3 working days of approving it. It then usually reaches you within 7–10 working days, depending on your bank or wallet.

6. What can’t be exchanged or refunded

  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery, or have had their tags removed.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after delivery.
